Business Problem

Before implementing a new feature, developers often spend several hours researching third-party platforms to determine:

Which platform best fits the project requirements?
Is it compatible with our technology stack?
How difficult is integration?
What are the pricing implications?
Are there API limitations?
What authentication methods are supported?
Are there SDKs available?
What risks should be considered?

This research is typically repeated across multiple projects, leading to duplicated effort and inconsistent decisions.

This workflow automates the entire evaluation process and produces an implementation-ready technical research document.
