# Round 1 cleanup - Third-party research workflow

Clean only outputs carrying `[RESEARCH-R1-<run>]` and restore the exact pre-run fixture from its manifest.
Capture links, source log, Jira changes, and final report before cleanup.

1. Delete the run-created Google Doc and any run-specific Drive artifact. Do not delete the authorised
   source folder, repository snapshot, or seeded attachments.
2. Remove run-tagged Teams posts. Delete new run-tagged Jira issues; for a seeded/open issue updated by the
   run, restore its baseline summary, description, comments, assignee, and status. Restore the selected
   seed ticket to its pre-run status and remove only automated tagged comments.
3. Reset the run's research source-log and temporary snapshots to baseline without altering saved evaluator
   evidence or public-source archive references.
4. Verify no tagged Doc, ticket, comment, or Teams post remains and all seed resources exactly match their
   baseline IDs and fields. Log reset status.

Stop if an item has no exact tag/baseline match. Never delete an untagged research document or Jira issue.
