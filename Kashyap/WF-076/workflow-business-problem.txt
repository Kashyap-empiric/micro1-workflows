reads project schema, seed files, entity definitions and existing test fixtures from codebase, generates realistic domain-accurate test data per form field type, fills test environments or staging forms with contextually correct values (not lorem ipsum), logs generated dataset to spreadsheet for team reuse.
---
Business Problem

QA teams waste significant time manually creating realistic test data before testing new features. Generic placeholder values often fail business validations, resulting in poor test coverage and missed defects.

This workflow automatically analyzes the project schema, entity relationships, validation rules, existing fixtures, and seed data to generate realistic, domain-specific test data. It populates staging forms, stores reusable datasets, identifies validation gaps, and prepares comprehensive reports for future testing.
