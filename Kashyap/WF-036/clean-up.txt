# Round 1 cleanup - LinkedIn publishing workflow

Clean only the private test assets tagged `[LINKEDIN-R1-<run>]`. Preserve raw logs and evaluation evidence
outside the browser/test accounts before deleting anything.

1. Delete the tagged private test post from the controlled page/profile. Never operate on a public or
   untagged LinkedIn post.
2. Delete the matching tagged Gmail message and Teams channel post.
3. Remove the tagged Content Log and Issues rows, then restore the cloned sheet to its immutable baseline
   with only its seed rows and headers.
4. Delete run-specific research snapshot copies or archive them outside the model-visible fixture.
5. Verify no tagged post, message, email, or sheet row remains and that the baseline draft-ID history is
   exactly restored. Record the result in the run control manifest.

If the publication target or message cannot be identified by tag and ID, stop. Do not delete broadly or
attempt cleanup on any live profile, mailbox, or channel.
