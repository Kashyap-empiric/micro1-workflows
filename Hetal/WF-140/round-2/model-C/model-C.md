## 2. Task accuracy, ignoring speed

**Rating:** 5/7

Both planted traps get caught, a renewal sitting exactly on the freeze cutoff and a whiteboarding tool quote converted from a foreign currency, and every number I checked against the source lands correctly. The grouped project management opportunity also gets correctly tested as a combined figure. The record column for retained standards is not applied consistently though, three carry a plain note that no ticket applies while a fourth adds a reference back to a different ticket for no clear structural reason. One ticket's added planning notes separately describe a next cycle migration using language more definite than the review actually authorised, which was analysis and planning only.

## 3. Efficiency

**Rating:** 5/7
**End-to-end time (minutes):** 6
**Wrong actions / recovery:** none, every step landed on the first try
**Commentary:** Source review, reconciliation, ticket filing, the tracker write back, and the channel post all follow in a straight line without much circling back. The five department drafts were not created together though, two went in first and the remaining three followed as a second round instead of one pass covering all five. ## 4. Writing quality

**Rating:** 5/7

The channel summary reads clearly, a short basis line up front followed by grouped headers for the filed opportunities, the higher risk calls, and the tracker only item, so a reader can scan it in seconds. The Gmail drafts read naturally, each closing with the required unverified address line, without sounding boilerplate. The Jira ticket bodies run long though, a planning scope section stacked on top of the required tools, target, and saving makes each one longer than it needs to be for something meant to be actioned quickly. The channel post also restates the same dollar figures twice, once in the opportunity list and again in the risk section, adding nothing new the second time.

The added planning recommendations in the tickets are also written in the same confident, cited tone as the sourced figures, so nothing visually separates a grounded number from the model's own suggested next step.

## 1. Overall task success

**Rating:** 5/7

Every figure in the tracker matches what the tickets, the live post, and the drafts show, once I check each of them against the real source myself, and nothing was guessed where the policy gave no answer. What holds this at 5/7 rather than higher is that the tickets carry planning content past what this review was actually authorised to produce, stated with more certainty than an analysis and planning scope supports, alongside a small consistency gap in how retained standards get recorded.
