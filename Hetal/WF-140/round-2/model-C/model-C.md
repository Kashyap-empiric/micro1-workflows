## 2. Task accuracy, ignoring speed

**Rating:** 5/7

Every number I checked against the source lands correctly, including both planted boundary cases, the renewal sitting exactly on the freeze cutoff and the quote converted from a foreign currency for the complementary whiteboarding tool, and the grouped project management opportunity is correctly tested as a combined figure. Two things keep this off a higher number. ## 3. Efficiency

**Rating:** 5/7

**Wrong actions / recovery:** none, no failed step or redo anywhere in the run
**Commentary:** This moved in a straight line from source review through reconciliation, ticket filing, the tracker write back, and the channel post without circling back on anything.
