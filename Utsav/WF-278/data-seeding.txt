2) Make a Google Doc called "Vantridge - Outside Counsel Guidelines" in my Google Drive, with exactly
this text:

"VANTRIDGE INDUSTRIES. OUTSIDE COUNSEL BILLING GUIDELINES, IN FORCE 01 JANUARY 2026. Fictional
company, invented for testing. These guidelines are the authoritative statement of Vantridge's
billing rules for this exercise. Treat them as correct and complete, and do not rely on outside
knowledge of any real company's guidelines or of any billing system's defaults.

1.1 SCOPE. Every fee line and every disbursement line on an invoice from outside counsel is reviewed
against these guidelines before the invoice is paid. Nothing on an invoice is paid until every line
on it has been dispositioned.

2.1 CLOSED MATTERS. We do not pay time or disbursements recorded against a matter after the closure
date shown for that matter on the matter schedule. A line dated after the closure date is not
approved and it is not reduced. It goes to the Vantridge matter supervisor named for that matter,
because either the work was asked for and the matter was closed too early, or it should not have been
done at all, and the invoice cannot tell us which of those it is.

3.1 STAFFING. Work is done at the lowest level that can do it properly. A line billed at partner
rates for work an associate could have done is reduced to the associate rate of $425.00 an hour. The
hours are left alone and only the rate moves.

3.2 COMPLEX WORK. This is the other half of 3.1 and it matters as much. Where a task is complex or
novel enough that it genuinely needs a partner's judgment, we would rather a partner did it than see
it pushed down to an associate who will take three times as long and get to a worse answer. On that
work the partner rate is the cost-effective answer and it is what we are asking for. Do not reduce
the rate on a line like that, do not reduce its hours, and do not treat the seniority of the
timekeeper as a finding on its own. Approve it.

3.3 RATE CARD. The agreed rates for Orbison & Slate on hourly matters are partner $875.00, senior
associate $595.00, associate $425.00, paralegal $215.00. A line billed above the agreed rate for the
timekeeper's title is reduced to the agreed rate. A matter under an alternative fee arrangement bills
at the rate shown against it on the matter schedule and not at these rates.

4.1 BLOCK BILLING. Each time entry records one task. An entry that runs two or more separate tasks
together into a single block of time is rejected in full, and the firm rebills the tasks as separate
entries. The entry is the only way we can test whether a task was worth the time it took and whether
it was billed at the right level, and a block hides both.

4.2 BLOCK BILLING ON A MATTER UNDER AN ALTERNATIVE FEE ARRANGEMENT. Section 4.1 does not apply to a
matter shown on the matter schedule as being under an alternative fee arrangement. Every timekeeper
on those matters bills at the one agreed rate, so the entry cannot tell us anything about the level a
task was billed at, which is half of what 4.1 is for, and we priced the rest of it when we agreed the
arrangement. An entry that combines tasks on an AFA matter is not rejected under 4.1 and it is not
reduced under it either. Approve it.

4.3 TRAVEL. Travel time is billed at 50 per cent of the timekeeper's rate. Where a line is billed at
the full rate for travel, take half of it off. Travel time is billed at the full rate only where the
timekeeper worked on the matter while travelling and the narrative says what that work was.

5.1 LEGAL RESEARCH. We pay for legal research on our matters where the Vantridge matter supervisor
authorised the research in advance, and the narrative on the line names the person who authorised it
and the date they did. Both limbs are required and the line has to carry both. Where the research was
authorised but the narrative does not name the approver, the line is rejected and the firm rebills it
with a narrative that does. We do not go back through our own files to reconstruct an approval that
the narrative should have stated.

6.1 EXPERTS, CONSULTANTS AND VENDORS. The firm engages an expert, a consultant or a vendor on a
Vantridge matter only with our written approval, obtained before the engagement. A line for an
engagement made without that approval is rejected in full.

6.2 EXPERTS ENGAGED IN AN EMERGENCY. Section 6.1 does not reach an engagement where a court deadline
or an order left no time to get approval first and the Vantridge matter supervisor for the matter
could not be reached, provided the firm told us about the engagement within two business days of
making it. An engagement inside 6.2 is approved and is not reduced for want of prior approval.

7.1 DISBURSEMENTS WE DO NOT PAY. These are the firm's overhead and are never reimbursable, whatever
the matter and however they are charged: online legal research subscriptions and database access,
whether billed as a subscription, an allocation or a per-search charge; secretarial and
administrative time; word processing; overtime for the firm's staff; local travel and meals for the
firm's own people; office supplies; general photocopying and printing.

7.2 DISBURSEMENTS WE DO PAY. Court filing and hearing fees. Deposition and hearing transcripts. Fees
charged by an expert, a consultant or a vendor engaged under section 6. Long-distance travel at
economy fare. Service of process. These are reimbursed at cost with no mark-up."
